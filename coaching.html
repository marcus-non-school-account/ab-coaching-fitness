What the gym work is for

Three qualities,
trained on purpose.

None of this is bodybuilding, and none of it is conditioning for its own sake. Every block is aimed at something that shows up in a match.

Explosive

Rate of force. The difference between attempting a takedown and finishing it before the reaction. Trained with speed work, jumps and heavy compounds moved fast, not slow grinding reps.

Powerful

Absolute strength through positions you actually fight from. Grips that hold to the buzzer, a frame that does not collapse, and enough force to make your size an advantage instead of a limit.

Durable

Hips, shoulders and spine loaded through the ranges jiu-jitsu puts them in, so being folded up stops costing you weeks. Availability is the quality that decides careers.

In person · Dublin

2-1 partner training

The same session split between two people. It suits training partners and teammates prepping for the same competition, and it brings the cost per person down considerably.

Sharing a slot does not mean sharing a plan. Two athletes with different injuries and different competition dates get two different programmes in the same hour — you just work through them alongside each other. Cancelling on a coach is easy; cancelling on your training partner is harder, and attendance is the single biggest predictor of whether a block works.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

I already train five or six times a week. Where does this fit?

Two or three gym sessions, placed so they do not blunt your hardest mat days. You are not adding volume on top of a full week — the plan is built around what you already do.

Will lifting make me slow or heavy?

Not if it is programmed for your sport. The work is aimed at force and speed rather than size, and if you are competing in a weight class, holding that weight is part of the brief.

I have a competition in ten weeks. Is that enough time?

Yes, and it changes the plan. Ten weeks is a real block: heavy early, sharper as it gets close, and backed off in the last week so you arrive fresh rather than fried.

I have an injury. Should I wait until it settles?

Usually not. Most competitors arrive with something. Augusto will work around it and rebuild the area rather than have you sit and wait. If it needs a physio first, he will tell you.

Do I need a full gym?

No. Online programmes are written for the equipment you actually have. Tell him what is in your gym and the plan is built from that list.

How many athletes does he take on?

A small number, and slots open a few at a time. If the month is full he will say so rather than stretch himself thin across too many people.
